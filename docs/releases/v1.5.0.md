# Luffy Create 1.5 — type the edit

The biggest Luffy Create update yet. Describe an edit in plain words and it happens. Cinematic transitions, 43 effects, speed ramps, precise background removal, jump cuts and add-ons for explainer videos are all new. Everything runs on your own computer: free, open source, no watermark, and fast on low-end PCs.

## Highlights

- 🤖 **AI Agent: type the edit.** "Remove the pauses and punch in on the cuts", "neon outline on me, phonk on the beat", "slow-mo start", "make all scenes 3 seconds". You see the plan before anything changes, and every AI edit can be undone by its number.
- ✂️ **Jump cuts and edit-by-transcript.** Cut pauses, filler words ("um", "uh") and retakes automatically, or delete words in the transcript and the video cuts itself.
- 🎬 **22 cinematic transitions** on one GPU engine: whip pan, zoom blur, light leak, film burn, glitch, cube and more, plus an intro from black and a fade-out ending.
- ✨ **43 effects in 9 groups:** neon outline and aura around a person, VHS, 8mm, glitch, warp, light leaks, lens flare, phonk, rain, snow and 12 colour looks. Effects can hit on the music's beat.
- 🏎️ **Speed ramps and freeze frames:** slow-mo start, montage, hero, bullet time and more, or draw your own curve. The sound follows the speed and keeps its pitch.
- 🪄 **Precise background removal.** One click removes, blurs or colours the background of a photo or video, with no green screen. It works on people, cars, products and groups. Click a person or object to keep only it, or to remove it.
- 🧩 **Add-ons for explainers:** neural network with forward pass, flowchart, code walkthrough, equation reveal, title card, lower third and hook/CTA.

## 🤖 AI Agent

- **Plain-language editing.** Scenes, text, shapes, media, animations, transitions, effects, speed ramps, backgrounds, layout, captions and add-ons can all be edited by typing. The built-in parser is instant and works offline.
- **See the plan first.** The agent shows what it will do before it changes anything.
- **Undo any edit.** `/history` lists the project's AI edits, and `/undo #N` reverts one of them while keeping later changes.
- **Point at things.** `@` mentions your media and `/` inserts an add-on by name.
- **Smart edits that listen to your media:**
  - remove silences;
  - remove filler words;
  - remove retakes;
  - cut to the beat;
  - split a long recording into shots;
  - duck the music under your voice.
- **Placing clips:** "make video 1 full screen and video 2 bottom right", "put this in the upper half".

## ✂️ Talking-head and UGC editing

- **Jump-cut studio:**
  - cuts pauses, filler words and retakes, with optional punch-in zooms so the cuts look deliberate;
  - previews every cut on the timeline before applying;
  - undo all of it in one step.
- **Edit by transcript:** click a word to jump to it, select words and press Delete to cut them, and bring them back the same way. The transcript is made by Whisper running on your computer.
- **Auto reframe:** turns landscape footage into 9:16 with the frame following the speaker's face. Choose Steady, Auto or Follow.
- **Split screen layouts:** side by side, stacked or grid in one click.
- **Caption presets** and **music ducking** under the voice.

## 🎬 Transitions

- **One GPU engine** draws transitions identically in the editor, Preview and export.
- **22 transitions** in Basic, Cinematic and Energy groups. These include whip pan, zoom blur, spin, blur dissolve, light leak, film burn, luma fade, flash, glitch, pixelate, stretch, slices and cube, alongside Morph, Flash Blur and Flicker Shake.
- **Intro from black** on the first scene, and an **Ending** fade-out to black or white.
- **Natural motion:** the outgoing scene keeps playing during the transition, and the sound crossfades.
- **Easy to choose:** hover a transition to preview it live. Each one has its own direction, strength, softness and easing.

## ✨ Effects, speed ramps and freeze frames

- **43 GPU effects in 9 groups:**
  - **Body (AI):** neon outline, aura glow, spotlight and echo trail around the person.
  - **Retro & Texture:** VHS, 8mm, old film, dust and scratches, paper, halftone comic, film grain.
  - **Glitch:** digital glitch, RGB split, CRT screen, signal loss.
  - **Warp:** wave, swirl, bulge, ripple, kaleidoscope, heat haze.
  - **Light:** glow, halation, light leaks, light sweep, god rays, lens flare, defocus, vignette.
  - **Energy:** phonk, strobe, zoom pulse, handheld shake, flash, light flicker.
  - **Motion:** float, wiggle, drift, Ken Burns.
  - **Weather:** rain, snow.
  - **Colour looks:** cinematic, teal & orange, vintage, noir, cyberpunk and 7 more.
- **Building effects:**
  - **Hover to preview** an effect on your own clip, then stack as many as you like.
  - **Timing:** each effect can run for the whole clip or from/to a time, with fades. Timed effects show as bars on the timeline that you can drag and resize.
  - **Beat sync:** effects marked ♪ hit on the beat of your music.
  - **Surprise me** builds a random combination.
- **Speed ramps:** 7 presets (Slow-mo start, Montage, Hero, Bullet, Flash in, Flash out, Jump), or drag the speed curve yourself. The sound follows the speed and keeps its pitch.
- **Freeze frame:** hold any moment, then carry on.
- **Old projects:** their grading, grain, vignette and media effects open in the new effects system.

## 🪄 Background removal (Edit BG)

- **Edit BG** in the left menu has one-click **Remove**, **Blur** (portrait look), **Colour background**, **Green screen** and **Original**.
- **Precise cut-out:** a one-time 44 MB download.
  - **Coverage:** clean edges on people, cars, products and groups.
  - **Speed:** photos take about a second. Videos are processed in the background, smoothed so edges don't flicker, and remembered, so each clip is only processed once.
- **Choose what stays:** press **Keep** or **Remove**, then click a person or object on the canvas. This is a one-time 45 MB download. On video, click any frame and the subject is followed through the clip.
- **Low-end friendly:** it runs on your computer's CPU, so it works on low-end laptops too, and it stops using memory when you're done.
- **Green screen:** automatic colour detection, spill removal and edge softness.
- **Cut clips:** changes apply to every part of a clip that was cut into parts.

## 🧩 Add-ons, diagrams and animation

- **Add-ons** you can add from the menu or by typing `/` in the AI Agent:
  - Neural network, which animates its forward pass;
  - Flowchart / pipeline;
  - Code walkthrough;
  - Equation reveal;
  - Title card;
  - Lower third;
  - Hook / CTA.
- **Editing add-ons:** they're editable by prompt ("make the network five layers"), and a whole add-on can be put in perspective.
- **Connectors:** arrows that stay attached to shapes as you move them. Connect items as a chain, column to column (a network layer in one click) or as a hub.
- **Group Effects:** animate items one after another as waves (left → right, top → bottom, rows or columns together), repeat a wave, or start one after the last ends.
- **Groups and locking:** Ctrl+G and Ctrl+Shift+G; a locked group moves as one.

## 🗂️ Workflow

- **Content Planner** (title bar): keep video ideas, scripts and to-dos. They're available in every project and saved automatically.
- **Export:**
  - asks where to save before rendering;
  - keeps full speed when the window is minimised or covered;
  - **9:16 projects now export at full 1080×1920.**
- **Settings:** an About page, and a feedback link.
- **Faster:** smoother timeline rendering, fewer re-renders and a refreshed look for pop-up windows.

## 🐛 Fixes

- **Portrait export size:** vertical (9:16) videos exported at "1080p" came out at 608×1080, and the export window showed the wrong size. They now export at 1080×1920.
- **Sound in speed-ramped clips:** the exported sound of a speed-ramped clip drifted ahead of the picture. Each sound piece is now cut to its exact length, so freezes are silent and the sound stays in sync.
- **Transitions:** the editor, Preview and export now draw push, wipe direction and zoom the same way. The zoom was nearly invisible and wipe edges were hard; both are fixed.
- **Panels and scrubbing:** Edit BG no longer jumps to the Video panel, and scrubbing while paused switches scenes correctly.
